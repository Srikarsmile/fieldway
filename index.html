What is this?

A space for Fieldway to come together

Friends of Fieldway is a community organisation for local residents of all ages in Fieldway, Croydon. We are developing opportunities to meet neighbours, learn new skills, and support one another, with residents helping shape what comes next.

   

Why does this matter?

Because everyone deserves to feel…

Seen

Known by name, noticed when you walk through the door.

Included

Whoever you are, wherever you're from, you're welcome here.

Part of something

Whether you've lived here for years or just arrived, this is for you.

Activities we're planning

Allotments, games, art, family fun, and community trips are ideas we would like to develop with residents. These activities are not yet scheduled. Tell us what you would enjoy or how you could help.

Photos illustrate the kinds of activities we have in mind. Dates will be announced once they are confirmed.

Help shape our activities
Positive Life Courses in development

Sleep, food and drink

We started Friends of Fieldway to help neighbours feel connected and supported. With our confirmed partner Positive Life, we are planning introductory talks about sleep, food and drink.

Sleep: an introduction to everyday sleep habits and wellbeing. Food and drink: an introduction to how everyday food and drink choices relate to wellbeing.

Course dates and details will be confirmed. Contact us to register your interest.
